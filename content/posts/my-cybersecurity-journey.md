## Why I Started This Blog

Welcome to JO3INGR Security Notes.

I created this blog to document my development
as a cybersecurity student, aspiring in
penetration tester and Vulnerability research.

I am interested in web security, penetration
testing, vulnerability research, and digital
forensics.

## What You Will Find Here

I plan to document my practical labs, technical
experiments, cybersecurity tools, challenges,
and lessons learned and writeups.

My goal is to improve my understanding by
practising, documenting my findings, and
sharing useful knowledge with other learners.

I am still learning, and this blog will grow
alongside my skills.

One lab, one vulnerability, and one lesson
at a time.

consistency is the Key!
